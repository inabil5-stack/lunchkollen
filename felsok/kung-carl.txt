    Today's Lunch
Take a break in the city and settle down for lunch at Kung Carl. Every week, the kitchen presents a new lunch menu with three dishes to choose from – one with meat, one with fish, and a vegetarian option, made with seasonal ingredients and lots of flavor.
The menu also includes our regular favorites, and every Thursday, we keep the tradition alive with classic pea soup and pancakes. On Fridays, we round off the week with the meat of the week, fries, and KC béarnaise.
Perfect for a quick lunch, a longer meeting over food, or just a really good break in the middle of Stureplan.
